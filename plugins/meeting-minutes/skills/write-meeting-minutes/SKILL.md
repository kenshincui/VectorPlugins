# 会议纪要

## Workflow

1. Establish meeting title, date, participants, purpose, and source completeness from evidence. Mark missing fields as `待补充`.
2. Separate what was discussed from what was decided. A proposal, suggestion, or unresolved debate is not a decision.
3. Extract action items only when an actual commitment exists. Record owner, due date, dependency, and acceptance condition when stated.
4. Resolve names consistently across aliases and speakers; do not assign an owner based only on who mentioned a task.
5. Produce the requested level of detail and preserve links or timestamps for important evidence when available.

## Default Chinese format

1. 会议结论
2. 关键讨论
3. 决策事项
4. 行动项 table: 事项 | 负责人 | 截止时间 | 状态/依赖
5. 风险与分歧
6. 待确认

Keep personal opinions, uncertain transcription, and off-topic chatter out of confirmed decisions. If audio transcription is requested but no transcription engine is available, report that constraint instead of fabricating a transcript.
